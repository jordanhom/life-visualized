# Project Brief: Life Visualized

## Purpose

- Help users reflect on the scale of a lifetime by visualizing a statistical lifespan estimate from a timezone-free birthdate and static US CDC 2021 life-table data.

## Scope

- In scope: a static browser SPA with birthdate/sex input, estimate output, and Weeks (Age), Weeks (Calendar), Months, and Years grids.
- Boundaries: no prediction or medical guidance; no backend, accounts, telemetry, input transmission, persistence, runtime dependencies, or production build step.

## Success

- A user can complete a valid calculation, understand its statistical limits, explore all four accessible responsive views, and reset cleanly while their data remains in the browser.
