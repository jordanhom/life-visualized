# Product Context

## Users And Problems

- People seeking a tangible, sensitive way to reflect on finite time.
- Lifespan scale and statistical expectancy are abstract; a block grid makes them visible while avoiding claims of individual prediction.

## Proposed Experience

- Accept birthdate and biological sex, progressively reveal the estimate and grid, support four synchronized perspectives, explain colors and axes, and provide a clean Start Over path.

## Product Principles

- Privacy by default: user inputs remain in browser memory.
- Reflection, not prediction: preserve the disclaimer and sensitive tone.
- Accessibility and responsive behavior are part of the maintained product contract.
- User-facing current-date behavior follows the browser's local calendar.
